What if orchestration could be written as a pure function? The somewhat steep learning curve of modern workflow engines, either in terms of programming model, deployment aspects or testability can put a damper on writing them and obfuscate the very process they are trying to automate. In this talk you'll learn about a simple, readable and testable way to write them, with full retention of the decisions made over time, using a practical example as our guide. We'll touch on simple patterns to help one deal with failure, time, and continuation.
