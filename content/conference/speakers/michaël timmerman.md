 Hi, I'm Michaël. I’ve always been the kind of person who likes to understand how things work, especially when it involves computers and technology. My journey started with a degree in Electromechanical Engineering, but after my studies I discovered that software was where my real interest was.

After several years working as a full-stack developer, I moved into the world of DevOps and quickly became fascinated by everything around cloud-native technologies, Kubernetes, and modern software delivery. What started as an interest in automation and infrastructure evolved into a specialization in building platforms that help teams deliver software better.

Over the years, I realized that a well-designed platform can have a huge impact: not only by making infrastructure easier to consume, but by improving the entire software delivery process, ranging from development and security to operations and governance.

Today, I work as a Platform Architect, helping customers design and build modern cloud-native platforms for enterprise and distributed edge environments. Although my role has become more architectural, I still like to stay hands-on as much as possible: experimenting with new technologies, building solutions, and understanding the details behind the platforms I design.
